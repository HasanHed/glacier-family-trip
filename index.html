Wildlife viewing

Logan Pass

Mountain goats and bighorn sheep are possible. Never approach or feed wildlife.

Many Glacier

Good chance for moose near wet areas and bears at a distance. Carry bear spray and keep the child close.

St. Mary valley

Watch open slopes and lake edges in early morning and evening.

Safety: Keep at least 100 yards from bears and wolves and 25 yards from other wildlife. Do not let a child run ahead on trails.

September weather & backup plan

Expect large temperature swings: cool mornings, possible rain or snow at higher elevations, and rapid changes at Logan Pass. Carry fleece, rain shells, warm hats, and gloves every day.

ConditionBest adjustment
Clouds at Logan PassSwap Day 2 with the clearest day.
Alpine road closureUse US-2 around the south side and focus on Lake McDonald or Many Glacier.
Heavy rainTrail of the Cedars, visitor centers, lodges, short pullouts, and a boat tour if operating.
Wildfire smokeUse the side with better air quality; avoid long exertion with a child.
